## 🎯 About Me

I'm a final-year engineering student at **École Hassania des Travaux Publics (EHTP)** in Casablanca (GIS & Data Science track), working at the intersection of **Data Science, Generative AI, Geospatial Analysis, and Software Engineering**. I like building systems that turn raw, messy data into something usable: modeled, mapped, simulated, or served through a clean API or interface.

- **Data & ML:** classification pipelines with Scikit-learn, neural networks (MLP and CNN, including backpropagation from scratch), computer vision with YOLOv8, and Geo-AI for flood-risk prediction in Greater Casablanca.
- **GenAI & LLM engineering:** RAG applications with LangChain, and an LLM-driven service that generates JMeter performance tests from OpenAPI specifications (done during my internship at Banque Centrale Populaire).
- **Software & Cloud:** full-stack apps with **Spring Boot** and **ASP.NET Core**, REST APIs with **FastAPI**, and CI/CD on AWS with **Docker, Terraform, CodePipeline and Jenkins**.
- **Risk modeling:** network-based simulation of how a cyber incident propagates through a financial system and what it costs in CET1 and LCR (synthetic data).

**Open to:**
- 🎓 A **6-month PFE (Projet de Fin d'Études) internship starting February 2027**: Data Science, ML, GenAI or Data/Cloud Engineering
- 🤝 Collaborations on Data Science, Geo-AI and GenAI projects
- 💬 Conversations about ML pipelines, LLM applications, spatial data or cloud architecture

<summary><b>🏦 Cyber Stress Test of a Synthetic Financial System</b></summary>

Network-based simulation of systemic cyber risk: how an incident spreads through a financial system and what it costs in solvency and liquidity. All data is synthetic and thresholds are illustrative.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Stack** | Python, NetworkX, NumPy, Pandas, Jupyter Notebook |
| **Model** | Directed graph of 16 actors and 38 dependencies; PageRank, betweenness and single points of failure |
| **Simulation** | Monte-Carlo propagation on 3 attack scenarios mapped to MITRE ATT&CK |
| **Impact** | Outages translated into CET1 and LCR effects per bank; 2 mitigation measures tested (up to 69% reduction in the expected number of banks below threshold in the most severe scenario) |
| **Repository** | [cyber-stress-test](https://github.com/SihamBouzagrar/cyber-stress-test) |

</details>
